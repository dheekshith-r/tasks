## Direction contract
THESIS: A to-do list as a post-office sorting frame: one typed line is postmarked and dropped into its day's slot. It refuses the hero-plus-screenshot page and the sidebar-plus-list app.
OWN-WORLD: White ground; ink frame of 3px rules forming pigeonhole slots with a soft inner shadow; yellow label strips on each slot lip; white paper slips; a red circular postmark with the date; Overpass and Overpass Mono; square corners.
STORY: The visitor sees their own week sorted into slots, types one line, sees it postmarked into the right slot, and opens their list.
