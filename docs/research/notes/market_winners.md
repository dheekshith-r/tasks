# Personal to-do app market winners (as of November 2026)

All "checked 2026-11-12" values come from live pages read on that date. Store ratings are US App Store values. Google Play pages did not render in the fetch tool, so Play ratings and download bands are UNKNOWN for every app.

## Q1. Per-app fact sheet: platforms, pricing, business model, users, ratings

### Takeaway
Todoist and TickTick lead the paid freemium segment. Both have about 4.8 to 4.9 stars and claim very large user bases. Free bundled apps from platform owners (Microsoft To Do, Google Tasks, Apple Reminders) have the largest rating counts. Things 3 wins on design with a one-time purchase. Any.do competes with heavy discounting.

## Q2. Recurring "winning ideas" across the leaders

### Takeaway
Six ideas recur: fast natural-language capture, sync on every platform, deep ecosystem integration, calendar plus planning views, AI capture and agent access, and a generous free tier with a clear upgrade line. Design quality with a one-time price is a seventh path that only Things uses at scale.

### Cited Findings
1. **Natural-language quick add.** Todoist parses date, project (#), label (@), priority (p1-p4) and recurrence in one line. — [Todoist help](https://todoist.com/help/articles/115001745265), [The Sweet Setup](https://thesweetsetup.com/using-natural-language-with-todoist/). Apple calls Todoist a "lightning-fast task manager" that makes complex to-dos "unusually easy" to enter. — [App Store: Todoist](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/todoist-to-do-list-planner/id572688855) (checked 2026-11-12)
2. **Every platform, synced.** Todoist ships on iOS, Android, macOS, Windows and web. — [TechCrunch 2026-01-21](https://techcrunch.com/2026/01/21/todoists-app-now-lets-you-add-tasks-to-your-to-do-list-by-speaking-to-its-ai/)
3. **Ecosystem integration.** Microsoft To Do wins on "deep integration with Microsoft's ecosystem"; Google Tasks is "available through all your Google apps". December 2025. — [Zapier](https://zapier.com/blog/best-todo-list-apps/). Evidence it works: the top two rating counts among free apps (270K and 108K). — [App Store MS To Do](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/microsoft-to-do/id1212616790), [App Store Google Tasks](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/google-tasks-get-things-done/id1353634006)
4. **Calendar, timers and daily planning.** TickTick is "Best for embedded calendars and timers"; Any.do's "Plan my Day" makes users schedule tasks. — [Zapier](https://zapier.com/blog/best-todo-list-apps/). Todoist moved calendar layout and task duration into Pro. — [Todoist pricing](https://www.todoist.com/pricing). Sunsama, Akiflow and Motion build whole products on time-blocking. — [toolquestor](https://toolquestor.com/vs/sunsama-vs-motion). Motion's estimated $50M ARR supports demand for automated scheduling. — [Sacra](https://sacra.com/c/motion)
5. **Focus / habit features.** TickTick syncs focus time to Apple Health via HealthKit. — [App Store: TickTick](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/ticktick-to-do-list-calendar/id626144601). Highest star rating in the set (4.9). Other habit/streak evidence: UNKNOWN in this session.
6. **AI capture and AI agent access.** Todoist Ramble (voice to tasks): public beta 2025-11-19; 76,000 users ran about 290,000 sessions in three weeks; GA 2026-01-21 in 38 languages on Gemini 2.5 Flash Live. — [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/01/21/todoists-app-now-lets-you-add-tasks-to-your-to-do-list-by-speaking-to-its-ai/). Apple Reminders in iOS 26 suggests reminders from Mail and notes and auto-categorizes lists (Apple Intelligence devices only). — [9to5Mac](https://9to5mac.com/2025/06/17/heres-everything-new-in-reminders-with-ios-26/), [MacRumors](https://www.macrumors.com/guide/ios-26-notes-app-reminders-app/). Superlist MCP lets external AI assistants manage tasks (June 2026). — [Product Hunt](https://www.producthunt.com/products/superlist/launches/superlist-2)
7. **Generous free tier, clear upgrade line.** Todoist free: 5 projects, 3 filters; Pro unlocks reminders, calendar, AI. — [Todoist pricing](https://www.todoist.com/pricing). TickTick: "always free", Premium raises limits. — [App Store: TickTick](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/ticktick-to-do-list-calendar/id626144601)
8. **Design craft + one-time price.** Things 3: two Apple Design Awards, $9.99 one-time, 4.8 stars. — [App Store: Things 3](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/things-3/id904237743); Zapier "Best for elegant design". — [Zapier](https://zapier.com/blog/best-todo-list-apps/)

### Inferences
- AI value in 2026 sits in capture (voice, email, suggestions), not in auto-planning for mass-market apps. Auto-planning (Motion) sells to professionals at high prices.
- AI is a paywall lever: Todoist gates Task Assist and unlimited Ramble behind Pro.
- MCP / agent access is a new 2026 differentiator for task apps.

### Gaps
- No retention or conversion data for any idea (all evidence is ratings, reviews or usage counts).
- Habit/streak evidence for TickTick or others not sourced in this session.

## Q3. What changed in 2025-2026

### Takeaway
The big changes were AI capture features (Todoist Ramble, Apple Intelligence in Reminders), price increases (Todoist, likely TickTick), Superlist's MCP, and Amie's pivot to meeting notes. No major acquisition or shutdown of a leading personal to-do app was found.

### Cited Findings
- Todoist Ramble: beta 2025-11-19, GA 2026-01-21. — [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/01/21/todoists-app-now-lets-you-add-tasks-to-your-to-do-list-by-speaking-to-its-ai/)
- Todoist price rise reported December 2025. — [routine.co](https://routine.co/blog/posts/todoist-pricing); current $7/$5 Pro, $10/$8 Business. — [Todoist pricing](https://www.todoist.com/pricing) (checked 2026-11-12)
- TickTick now $49.99/year, $4.99/month vs $3.99/month in Zapier's December 2025 review. — [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/ticktick-to-do-list-calendar/id626144601), [Zapier](https://zapier.com/blog/best-todo-list-apps/)
- iOS 26 Reminders (2025): Apple Intelligence suggestions, auto-categorization, Control Center/Action Button capture, Liquid Glass design. — [9to5Mac](https://9to5mac.com/2025/10/13/heres-everything-new-in-reminders-with-ios-26/), [Neowin](https://www.neowin.net/news/exploring-ios-26-5-new-features-you-can-find-in-the-reminders-app/)
- Microsoft: Viva Goals retired 2025-12-31; Planner features removed January-February 2026; Project Online retires September 2026. To Do stays inside Planner. — [Neowin](https://www.neowin.net/news/microsoft-confirms-major-2026-update-to-remove-several-planner-features-add-new-ones/)
- Superlist: AI meeting notes May 2025; MCP June 2026. — [AlternativeTo](https://alternativeto.net/news/2025/5/task-management-app-superlist-introduces-ai-meeting-notes-with-transcript-and-chat), [Product Hunt](https://www.producthunt.com/products/superlist/launches/superlist-2)
- Amie: repositioned around AI meeting notes; free plan reportedly dropped (2026, conflicting). — [Ellie Planner](https://ellieplanner.com/comparisons/amie-calendar-review), [nocal.app](https://nocal.app/alternatives/amie)
- Google: AI task work went into Gemini Spark (May-October 2026), not Google Tasks. — [9to5Google](https://9to5google.com/2026/10/12/gemini-spark-task-redesign/)

### Inferences
- Platform owners (Apple, Google) are adding AI task features to their own assistants. This raises pressure on independent apps to win on capture speed and cross-platform reach.

### Gaps
- No acquisitions found for Amie, Superlist or Akiflow in 2025-2026 (aggregator data only; could lag).
- No confirmed shutdowns of a top personal to-do app in 2025-2026.

## Q4. Quick-add natural-language support

### Takeaway
Todoist has the richest and best-documented quick-add grammar. TickTick has smart date parsing plus tag and list symbols, but users rate it weaker. Data for the others is thin.

### Cited Findings
- **Todoist:** dates and times ("tomorrow 8pm"), recurrence ("every Monday", "every 3 days"; "every!" schedules from completion date), project #, label @, priority p1-p4, assignee +name. — [Todoist help](https://todoist.com/help/articles/115001745265), [Todoist help mirror on recurrence](https://get.todoist.help/hc/en-us/articles/360000636289), [The Sweet Setup](https://thesweetsetup.com/using-natural-language-with-todoist/), [usecarly](https://www.usecarly.com/blog/how-to-use-natural-language-in-todoist/). Ramble adds voice capture with spoken edits ("Actually, make that Thursday"). — [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/01/21/todoists-app-now-lets-you-add-tasks-to-your-to-do-list-by-speaking-to-its-ai/)
- **TickTick:** smart date parsing since v2.7.0 (2016, older), sets due date and reminder from text; option to remove parsed text. — [TickTick blog](https://blog.ticktick.com/2016/04/12/ticktick-270-a-new-breakthrough-with-city/). Symbols: #tag and ^list (2020 blog, older). — [curtismchale.ca](https://curtismchale.ca/2020/08/10/ticktick-quick-add-syntax). Reddit users report weaker parsing than Todoist (e.g. "second Tuesday"). — [r/ticktick mirror](https://axebps-redlib.hf.space/r/ticktick/comments/1im1vkt/smart_date_parsing_help)
- **Apple Reminders:** iOS 26 adds AI suggestions and auto-categorization, not a documented quick-add grammar. — [MacRumors](https://www.macrumors.com/guide/ios-26-notes-app-reminders-app/)

### Inferences
- A product that matches Todoist's grammar (dates, recurrence incl. completion-based, project, label, priority) plus voice capture meets the 2026 bar.

### Gaps
- Things 3, Microsoft To Do, Google Tasks and Any.do quick-add parsing: UNKNOWN (not verified in this session).
- TickTick priority symbol (!) and current list symbol: UNCONFIRMED.
