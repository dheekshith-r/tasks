# Magic moment (product flow step 4)

**Sentence:** The visitor types one plain line, for example "Pay rent friday !high #home", presses Enter, and sees it land on the right day, with the right priority and list, without a mouse, an account or a tour.

**Target:** under 10 seconds from first page load to first saved task, by keyboard only. A screen reader announces the save. An automated test checks it before launch (product flow step 6).

**Second moment (calm):** the next morning, yesterday's unfinished tasks wait quietly under "Carried over". There is no red count and no shame pile.

## What this means for the screens

- **Landing page:** the quick-add works in the first screen, with the visitor's real date. The page proves the magic moment instead of describing it.
- **Get started:** no sign-up (DEFINE.md section 11). Three optional steps protect the list: install, protect storage, back up or sync. "Skip and start typing" is always one key away.
- **App:** focus starts in the quick-add box. The first thing a new user can do is the magic moment.

## Taste lessons applied (~/.claude/taste.md)

- Real app first, in the first screen.
- One real thing: the visitor's own typed task and real date, parsed live (no made asset yet; that needs a source from Ddk).
- Boring chrome: standard nav, buttons, forms and footer.
- One move per direction.
- Sample data differs per direction and is labelled as a sample.
- No invented proof: quotes and the accessibility report are marked placeholders until they exist.
