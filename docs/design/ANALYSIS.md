# Reference analysis

Source: the 53 screenshots in `to-do-sample/references/` (10 sites). I opened each image. Mercury is one tall image; I cut it into 4 parts to read it.

Rule from `references/README.md`: copy the principles, not the style, art, typeface or layout. Each entry has 3 details that I saw and 1 principle. The principle IDs (P1 to P12) are listed at the end. `DIRECTIONS.md` uses these IDs.

## Cofounder

### cofounder-1-hero.png
- A fixed left column holds a small sunflower drawing at the top and the section nav lower down. The current item ("Cofounder") has a grey pill. The other items are light grey.
- The headline uses a pixel-cut serif display face at about 90px. The body text below it is a plain sans at about 20px.
- The hero art is a pixel-art landscape (sunflowers, lake, mountain). A frosted input box sits in its center. It shows a typed request ("Create an event for Tuesday at 2:00 PM…"), a text cursor and a black round send button.
- **Principle:** Put the real input of the product inside the art, so the first image is also a demo. (P2)

### cofounder-2-workflow.png
- A pixel meadow with orange poppies fills the panel. A frosted card on top shows a run: "Searching Notion · Completed", "Listing issues · Completed", "Adding content to Notion page · 92%".
- Each step has small chips with real item names ("Implement a user profile page", "Add scroll").
- The caption under the panel is two-tone: the first sentence is dark, the second sentence is grey.
- **Principle:** Show the product working step by step, with real states (done, in progress, a percent). (P2)

### cofounder-3-agent.png
- A pixel sky with cumulus clouds is framed by trees. A frosted "Todo List · 0%" card sits in the center with two items.
- The text sits in a separate right column. A 1px vertical rule and a 1px horizontal rule divide the art from the text.
- The section heading "Knowledgebase Agent" is a plain sans, not the display face.
- **Principle:** The art frames the product; the explanation stays in a calm column beside it. (P1)

### cofounder-4-integrations-stats.png
- 17 app icons sit in white rounded tiles, in rows. Each row has a hairline above and below it.
- The stats "59,191" and ">18 billion" use the pixel display face. The labels under them are small plain sans ("tasks automated by Cofounder").
- A customer quote sits beside a small square portrait. One phrase of the quote has a pale blue highlight ("Truly couldn't function without it").
- **Principle:** Keep the display face for headlines and numbers only; everything else is plain. (P6)

### cofounder-5-footer.png
- The footer repeats the hero headline, centered, with "by The General Intelligence Company" under it.
- A postage stamp with a pixel image of the New York skyline sits below, with "Made with ♥ in New York".
- The link list is a single plain column on the left. "Design by Altalogy" sits at the bottom right with an underline.
- **Principle:** End where you started: the closing repeats the hero line and adds one small human detail. (P11)

## Mercury

- All the feature visuals belong to one family of soft grey 3D forms: a light arc over a planet, a wire globe, overlapping glass circles, concentric rings with a lock, and toggles. The page has no stock icons.

## Kitchen.co

### kitchen-1-hero.png
- A centered black headline at very heavy weight: "Everything you do with clients, now in one place". A small bold eyebrow above it names the category ("Client Portal Software for Agencies").
- There is one orange-red button: "Get Started—it's FREE". The line under it is "No credit card required, cancel any time".
- Noise competes with the hero: a black promo bar with fire emoji and a chat bubble with a red badge. Do not copy this.
- **Principle:** Put the risk reversal directly under the main button. (P10)

### kitchen-2-features.png
- There are three black-ink drawings. The same character appears in each one, and the chef-hat brand mark is on his shirt, on a screen and on a shield.
- The drawings use solid black fills plus loose hatching. They have no color at all.
- Each block has a heavy centered title ("Centralized", "Asynchronous", "Safe & Secure") and centered body text. This is the 3-card template; copy the drawing system, not the layout.
- **Principle:** One recurring character plus the brand mark turns separate drawings into one system. (P1)

### kitchen-3-product-screen.png
- A real Google Doc ("Project Plan") is shown plainly inside a product frame with an "Embed link" bar and three avatar photos.
- The frame sits on a large warm-grey panel that bleeds off the left edge.
- The list on the right has a check mark, then a bold lead word, then plain detail: "**Docs.** Google Docs, SpreadSheets, Slides, and Forms."
- **Principle:** Lists read fast when each line starts with one bold word and then gives the plain detail. (P10)

### kitchen-4-integrations.png

- The "Integrations" eyebrow is small and bold. The headline "Works with your tools" is left-aligned, unlike the centered hero.
- The logo cards are white boxes with hairline borders on a warm grey ground.

### kitchen-5-final-cta.png
- Three characters walk over two hills toward a rising sun, past a signpost with two arrows.
- The closing headline has three lines and ends with "You'll thank us later."
- The button is the same orange-red "Get Started—it's FREE" as in the hero.
- **Principle:** The final call to action repeats the first one exactly and adds a human promise. (P10)

### kitchen-6-footer.png
- The logo sits beside a one-sentence product description ("The complete client portal software that lets you do it all…, stress-free").
- There are three link columns (Product, Solutions, Company) with bold black headings.
- The Solutions column names real audiences: "Law Firms", "Freelancers", "Accounting and Bookkeeping".
- **Principle:** The footer restates what the product is, in one sentence, for a visitor who scrolled straight to the bottom. (P11)

### kitchen-7-footer-bottom.png
- "© Kitchen.co 2026" and "Designed, developed and backed by 2create.io" sit on two plain lines.
- The legal links are in one row, divided by thin vertical pipes: Terms, Privacy, DPA, Cookie Policy, Cookie Settings.

## Zapier

### zapier-3-security-grid.png

### zapier-4-final-cta.png

### zapier-5-footer.png

## Linear

### linear-1-figures.png
- Each column has a mono label: "FIG 0.1", "FIG 0.2", "FIG 0.3".
- Each figure is a thin white isometric line drawing: stacked plates with the logo, a cluster of cubes, a fan of upright cards.
- Thin vertical rules divide the columns. The title is white and the description is grey.
- **Principle:** Label abstract ideas like figures in a technical manual. It makes them look precise and owned. (P8)

### linear-2-intake-product-ui.png
- The section head has two columns: a large title on the left ("Intake and integrations"), a paragraph and "Learn more →" on the right.

- The board shows real issue IDs ("ENG-926 Remove UI inconsistencies"), label chips, counts ("Todo 71"). It is cut off and fades at the right edge.

### linear-3-planning-product-ui.png
- A timeline with "MAR" and "APR" and day numbers shows two projects ("UI Refresh", "Split fares") with diamond milestones.
- One milestone has a red outline, and its bar continues as a red dashed overrun.
- A "Cycle time by agent" panel shows teal dot clusters with thin red trend lines.
- **Principle:** On a near-black page, color appears only inside data, where it means something. (P5)

### linear-4-customer-quotes.png

## Shared principles (IDs used in DIRECTIONS.md)

| ID | Principle | Seen in |
|---|---|---|
| P1 | One visual world (one art style, one mark) runs from hero to footer. | Cofounder, Mercury, Kitchen, Tailscale |
| P2 | The real product sits inside the hero art and works there. | Cofounder, Tailscale, Ramp, Linear |
| P3 | The visitor can try the product on the page. | Ramp 5, Cofounder 1 |
| P4 | Proof is specific and named: exact numbers, real people, a link to evidence. Logos stay quiet. | Linear, Tailscale, Ramp, Mercury |
| P5 | Restraint: one accent with one job; color only from the art or the data. | Linear, Tailscale, Kitchen, Zapier |
| P6 | A display face with character for headlines and numbers only; a plain face (often mono caps) for labels. | Cofounder, Tailscale, Ramp |
| P7 | Show the before and the after in one frame (the mess, the error, the input). | Ramp 6, Linear 2, Zapier 1 |
| P8 | Structure as a diagram: figure labels, joined grids, plain step diagrams. | Linear 1, Zapier 2–3, Tailscale 5 |
| P9 | Rhythm: dense and quiet sections alternate; one dark block closes the page. | Mercury, Zapier 4, Tailscale 6 |
| P10 | Plain, human copy: benefit in the button label, risk reversal under it, the final call repeats the first. | Kitchen, Ramp, Zapier |
| P11 | Small human and trust details: a place, a credit, legal and address, a closing that repeats the hero. | Cofounder 5, Kitchen 7, Ramp 8 |
| P12 | Much empty space, few typefaces, few colors. | All ten |
| P13 | One oversized, simple move beats many clever ones. | Figma 3, JetBrains 2, Kitchen 1 |
| P14 | One made asset with texture (photo, dither, ink, pixel, render) that no other company could ship. | JetBrains 1, Mercury, Cofounder, Kitchen |
| P15 | Without a made asset, real live data in the visitor's own locale does the same job. | Coinbase 1, 3 |

## What not to take

- Any art style, typeface, logo, layout or copy from these sites.
- Kitchen's promo bar, emoji and chat widget; the 3-card feature row.
- Glow and grainy gradient panels as decoration without a job (Zapier, Mercury). The design law bans glow and gradient slop.
- Invented proof. The app has no customer numbers or quotes yet, so the page uses only true facts (see DIRECTIONS.md).
