          Questions

          Is it really free?

          Yes. There is no paid plan and no trial, and it is MIT licensed. Sharing and teams are not here, because they need a server.

          Where are my tasks kept?

          In guest mode, in this browser on this device only. If you sign in with Google, they are kept in your own Firebase account, and only you can read them.

          Does it work offline?

          Yes, after you open it once. It installs like an app on a phone or a computer.

          What happens if I clear my browser data?

          Guest tasks are deleted with it. Save a backup file from the app's menu first; the app shows the date of your last backup.

          Can I use it on my phone?

          Yes, in your phone's browser, and you can add it to the home screen. To see the same list on your phone and laptop, sign in with Google.

          How do I move my tasks out?

          Export a backup file at any time and import it on another device. The file is yours.
