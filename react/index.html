          Your tasks live in six places. None of them talk to each other.

          A note on the fridge, a message to yourself, the back of a bus ticket. Tasks puts them in one list, on your own device.

          8:14 am

          One line. Three words it understands.

          Pay rent friday !high #home

          Friday

          Pay rent

          FRI!high#home

          1. fridayA day name becomes the date it is due. Today counts.
          2. !highHow much it matters gets a tag with the word on it.
          3. #homeThe list it goes to. A new name makes a new list.

          The line "Pay rent friday !high #home" becomes the task "Pay rent", due this Friday, high priority, in the list "home".

          Where your tasks are kept.

          No ads and no analytics. In guest mode the page makes no third-party requests; even the fonts come from this site.

          Guest mode, the default
          Your tasks are saved in this browser only. Nothing leaves your device. A backup is a file you save yourself.
          Optional Google sign-in
          Signed-in tasks are stored by Google Firebase so they sync to your other devices. Security rules let only your account read or write them, and you can delete them and your account from inside the app.
          Open source, built in the open
          Every line is on GitHub under the MIT license, with tests that run on every change. How AI helped, and how its work was checked, is in AI_WORKFLOW.md.
